Kawasaki Ridge UTV Accessories — Complete Buyer's Guide 2026

The Kawasaki Ridge debuted in 2024 as Kawasaki's most capable work/recreational side-by-side to date. With a 999cc three-cylinder engine, independent rear suspension, and a 1,500 lb payload rating, the Ridge sits squarely between a work utility machine and a recreational off-road vehicle. That versatility makes accessorizing it interesting — the same machine that runs fence lines also handles weekend trail riding.

Floor Protection: Start Here

The Ridge's cab floor takes a beating from boots, tools, and trail debris. OEM-spec TPE floor liners from brands like POKIAUTO and SAUTVS are precision-cut for the 2024-2026 Ridge footwell and drain channels. TPE outperforms rubber for cold-weather flexibility and is easier to clean than carpet. The 4-piece front/rear kits from POKIAUTO cover the entire cab without trimming.

Winch Mounts and Recovery Equipment

The Ridge's standard front bumper has no factory winch mount. JERBOR's steel powder-coat mount kit is specifically engineered for the Ridge and Ridge XR, includes all hardware, and accommodates most 3,000-4,500 lb winches. For a machine with this payload capacity, a winch is a near-essential tool upgrade. Pair it with a synthetic rope winch for best results — synthetic rope is safer than steel cable if it snaps under load.

Brushguard and Bumper Protection

Kawasaki's own OEM brush guard (part 99994-2094) is the clean factory-matching option for front protection. It mounts directly to the factory frame and protects the radiator and front fascia from brush impact. For more serious protection, aftermarket steel bumpers are starting to emerge as the Ridge platform matures and the accessory market catches up.

Visibility Upgrades

The Ridge's factory mirrors are adequate for road use but undersized for trail riding where side visibility matters more. Aftermarket rearview mirrors like the UTV wide-angle breakaway units give a much wider field of view. LED light bars and pod lights are popular add-ons — the Ridge's roof structure accepts standard light bar mounts without drilling.

Storage Solutions

With a 1,500 lb payload, the Ridge can carry significant cargo. Bed extenders, tailgate nets, cargo dividers, and lockable cargo boxes are all available in the growing Ridge accessory market. For the cab area, overhead cargo nets and console organizers maximize the usable interior space without reducing passenger comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best accessories for the Kawasaki Ridge UTV?

The most impactful first upgrades are floor liners (custom-fit TPE sets from POKIAUTO or SAUTVS), a winch mount for recovery capability, and upgraded rearview mirrors for better trail visibility. If you're running the Ridge off-road regularly, a front brush guard rounds out the essential protection package.

Do Kawasaki Ridge accessories from 2024 fit the 2025 and 2026 models?

Most accessories designed for the 2024 Ridge are compatible with 2025 and 2026 models since the platform remained largely unchanged. Always verify with the specific product listing — floor mats, winch mounts, and brush guards will list exact year compatibility.

Can I add a winch to a Kawasaki Ridge without modifying the bumper?

Yes — JERBOR and similar brands make direct bolt-on winch mount brackets specifically for the Ridge that use existing frame mounting points. No drilling or fabrication required. The bracket positions the winch centered on the front of the machine with the correct geometry for recovery use.

What size winch fits the Kawasaki Ridge?

A 3,000 to 4,500 lb winch is the appropriate range for the Ridge. The machine weighs approximately 1,800-2,000 lbs loaded, so a 3,000 lb winch provides adequate pulling capacity. Synthetic rope winches are safer than steel cable if the rope breaks under tension.

Are TPE floor mats better than rubber for the Kawasaki Ridge?

Yes for most uses. TPE stays flexible in cold temperatures (rubber stiffens and can crack), has a lighter weight, and cleans more easily. In extreme heat, TPE holds up as well as rubber. For a machine that sees four-season use in varied climates, TPE is the better floor material.

What's the difference between the Kawasaki Ridge and Ridge XR?

The Ridge XR is the higher-spec variant with more aggressive suspension tuning, standard factory accessories, and in some configurations a longer wheelbase. Accessory compatibility generally follows the same pattern — verify XR-specific fit for brackets, bumpers, and winch mounts as the mounting geometry may differ slightly.

Does the Kawasaki Ridge have a roof rack?

The Ridge's factory roof is a solid panel. Aftermarket roof racks and light bar mounts are becoming available as the platform matures, but the selection is smaller than for longer-established UTV platforms like Can-Am Maverick or Polaris RZR. Check that any roof mount is designed for the Ridge's specific roof geometry before purchasing.

What floor mat brand makes the best fit for the Kawasaki Ridge?

POKIAUTO's 4-piece set and SAUTVS's 2-piece front set are the two most verified options for precise fit on the 2024-2026 Ridge. Both use the specific footwell measurements for the Ridge Crew cab. Generic 'universal fit' mats will not conform to the raised floor channels and drainage grooves that the custom-fit options are designed around.
