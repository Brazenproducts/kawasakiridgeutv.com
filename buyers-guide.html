Kawasaki Ridge UTV Buyer's Guide — Where to Actually Start

The Kawasaki Ridge sits between a work machine and a recreational side-by-side. With a growing but still maturing accessory market, the hard part isn't finding parts — it's knowing what actually matters for how you use the machine. Here's the priority order we recommend.

Our recommended order: Floor protection first (cheap, immediate), winch mount second (essential recovery tool for a 1,500 lb-payload machine), visibility upgrades third, storage/cargo once you know what you're hauling, brush guard/bumper protection last.

1. Floor Protection — Cheapest, Most Obvious Upgrade

The Ridge's cab floor takes a beating from boots, tools, and trail debris. Custom-fit TPE liners are a bolt-on, no-tools upgrade with immediate benefit. See our floor protection picks.

2. Winch Mount — Add Recovery Capability

The Ridge's factory bumper has no winch mount, and this is a work-capable machine that will eventually get stuck. A direct bolt-on mount kit is a one-time investment that pays for itself the first time you need it. See our winch & recovery picks.

3. Visibility — Mirrors and Lighting

Factory mirrors are undersized for trail use, and the Ridge's roof accepts standard light bar mounts without drilling. Both are cheap upgrades with an outsized safety benefit. See our mirrors & lighting picks.

4. Storage — Once You Know What You're Hauling

Don't guess at cargo needs before your first few trips. A roll bar bag and console box cover most day-to-day needs; step up to a full overbed cargo rack once you've identified a specific gap. See our storage & cargo picks.

5. Brush Guard / Bumper Protection — Last

The most situational category — only worth it if you're regularly running brush, forest roads, or technical trail sections where front-end impact is a real risk. The genuine Kawasaki OEM brush guard is the clean factory-matching option. See our brush guard picks.
